I work as a teaching assistant for undergraduate statistics courses at the University of Michigan, working with small groups of students on core statistical concepts.

As part of this role, I design lesson materials on correlation and causation for undergraduate statistics students, with an emphasis on interactive, discussion-based learning. This has included:

* Lesson plans following structured evaluation rubrics
* LaTeX Beamer slide decks and accompanying speech scripts
* In-class activities using real data visualizations to illustrate the limitations of correlation as a measure of relationship strength
